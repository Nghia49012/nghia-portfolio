## Post 1: AI Visibility and Brand Radar

**Core Insight:** “AI discoverability” is becoming a distribution channel—map which prompts mention your competitors vs. you, then publish structured, long-form content that directly answers those queries so LLMs can cite you (especially for high-intent topics like welcome flows, abandoned cart, and tool comparisons).

I wanted to know how often AI actually recommends me when someone asks about email marketing. Here's what I found:

When someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, or Google's AI Overviews about email marketing, Mailchimp shows up 25.8 million times. Klaviyo shows up 4.6 million times. Omnisend shows up 2.7 million times. And I only show up about 6,700 times.

## Post 2: The 7 Rules of Conversion Copywriting

**Core Insight:** Conversion comes from *clarity + outcomes + trust*—B2B emails should translate features into lived value, lead with helpfulness, make the reader the hero, and pair logic (proof) with emotion (story) so the offer is understood in seconds.

People are ignoring your offer for a reason.

And it’s not the price.

It’s about understanding why people buy in the first place.

Here are 7 rules every marketer should live by:

1️⃣ People don’t buy products — they buy outcomes  
They don’t buy insurance → they buy peace of mind  
They don’t buy headphones → they buy focus  
They don’t buy services → they buy time  
👉 Always translate features into real-life value

2️⃣ The best marketing doesn’t feel like marketing  
If it feels like an ad, people scroll.  
If it feels like helpful advice, people listen.  
👉 Be useful before being persuasive

3️⃣ Give away real value for free  
Not fluff. Not generic tips.  
Actual insights people can use immediately.  
👉 Make them think: “If this is free… what’s paid like?”

4️⃣ Trust > tricks  
Shortcuts might get clicks.  
Trust builds businesses.  
👉 Help first. Sell second.

5️⃣ Your audience is the hero  
Stop talking about your product.  
Start talking about their problems, goals, and wins.  
👉 Make your brand the guide, not the spotlight

6️⃣ Logic convinces. Emotion converts.  
Data explains. Stories connect.  
👉 Use both if you want people to act

7️⃣ Clarity beats perfection  
People don’t buy the best product.  
They buy the one they understand fastest.  
👉 If it takes more than 5 seconds to get it, you’ve lost them

Great marketing isn’t louder.

It’s clearer, more human, and more relevant.

## Post 3: Retention and Clarity Principles

**Core Insight:** Strong lifecycle email programs win by doing fewer things better—focus on one audience + one problem + one message, optimize for actions (not vanity metrics), and prioritize retention; specificity and clarity compound across onboarding, activation, and expansion.

Most marketing doesn’t fail because of bad ideas.

It fails because it’s trying to do too much… for too many people.

Here are 6 simple principles that consistently outperform “complex strategies”:

Start smaller than you think  
Don’t try to reach everyone.  
Speak to one audience, solve one problem, own one message.  
Clarity scales. Confusion doesn’t.

Attention is useless without action  
Views don’t pay salaries.  
Clicks don’t build businesses.  
What matters is: what do people do after they see you?

Retention > Acquisition  
Getting a customer is expensive.  
Keeping one is profitable.  
The real growth happens after the first purchase.

Your customer is the hero  
Too many brands talk about themselves.  
The best ones position the customer at the center and act as the guide.  
People care about their story, not yours.

Clarity beats information  
More features ≠ better marketing.  
If people don’t understand it instantly, they won’t buy it.  
Simple sells.

If you’re for everyone, you’re for no one.  
Specificity builds connection.

The clearer your audience → the stronger your message → the higher your conversions.

The pattern is simple:

👉 Focus beats noise  
👉 Clarity beats complexity  
👉 Relevance beats reach

Most brands don’t need more tactics.

They need fewer… done better.
