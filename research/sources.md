# 10 Experts: B2B SaaS Email Marketing & Newsletters

**Selection Strategy:** To build an effective B2B playbook, I chose 10 different experts in B2B Email Marketing, who specialise in 5 different aspects: Deliverability, User Onboarding, Lifecycle Automation, Growth, and Copywriting.
