# 10 Experts: B2B SaaS Email Marketing & Newsletters

**Selection Strategy:** To build a highly technical and effective B2B playbook, I chose 10 different experts in B2B Email Marketing. I deliberately pivoted away from generic marketing advice to focus heavily on the underlying infrastructure, outbound systems, and authentic copywriting. 

These experts are categorized into 5 core pillars:

## 1. Deliverability & Inbox Placement
*You can't convert if you're in the spam folder. These experts focus on sender reputation and inbox placement.*

### 1.1. Jesse Ouellette
* **Role:** Founder at LeadMagic
* **Links:** [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jesseoue/)
* **Annotation:** Jesse is a heavy-hitter in B2B email deliverability. His content is highly technical, focusing on catch-all domains, agentic AI workflows, and managing sender reputation to ensure outbound SaaS campaigns land in the primary inbox.

### 1.2. Lauren Meyer
* **Role:** CMO at SocketLabs
* **Links:** [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lauren-meyer-emailgeek/) | [Youtube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC1661WlfEcgyM3nKnNx7Y1w)
* **Annotation:** Lauren breaks down the "Art and Science" of deliverability. She provides deep context on mailbox provider behaviors (like Gmail's open rate metrics) and the operational reality of fixing deliverability issues across teams.
