In this video, we will go over how to
set up mail integrations. Mail
integrations can be used for things like
notifying your customer of problems
being reported, and they can also be for
notifying groups of customers, giving
them a heads up for a new security
exploits that their service might be
vulnerable to. So, let's get started.
First, we'll want to go into the
settings by clicking on your username on
the right side of Abuse HQ.
Now select settings.
Next, we'll go over to the left. This is
where we'll click on the mail
notifications.
The first thing we need to do is to we
need to add an SMTP server. We do this
by clicking on the manage SMTP service
button.
Since we don't have an SMTP service set
up yet, we'll need to add one.
You'll see a popup where you'll need to
enter some basic information regarding
your SMTP service.
At the very bottom, you can test your
connection. You can also send a test
email to verify your configuration was
correct.
Now that that's set up, next we'll want
to create our mail templates. And we do
that by clicking on the create template
button.
What you'll see now is a blank template
with different fields that you can
customize.
At the very bottom, you'll notice that
there's an email templates and templates
variables link.
If at any time you need assistance
creating these mail templates or any of
the variables that can be included
within the mail template, click on
either of these links to learn more.
